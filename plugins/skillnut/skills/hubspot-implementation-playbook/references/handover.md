# Module 7 - Handover and governance

Load this for Module 7. The project is finished when the team can run the system without the implementer.

## 1. Sales team manual (outline)

Write it for the reps, in plain language, with screenshots from their own portal.

1. **How we use HubSpot.** What lives here, what doesn't, and the one rule: if it isn't in HubSpot, it didn't happen.
2. **Definitions.** Every lifecycle stage, lead status value, deal stage with its exit criterion, and the required reasons. One line each.
3. **From first enquiry to signed customer.** The path a lead takes, who owns each step, what moves it forward automatically and what the rep must do.
4. **New leads.** How leads are assigned, the response deadline, the first-contact task, what to do with after-hours leads.
5. **Logging.** How to log calls and call outcomes, meetings, notes and LinkedIn touches.
6. **No-shows and reschedules.** What to do, what to log, when to recycle to nurturing.
7. **Deals.** The naming convention, creating a deal, moving stages, required fields, close dates, closed lost reasons.
8. **Managing your book.** Saved views for new leads, open deals and overdue tasks; daily and weekly routine.
9. **Data rules.** Don't create duplicates (search first), don't set lifecycle by hand, don't edit locked source fields, how to flag a bad record.
10. **Getting help.** Who the data steward is, how to request a new field, workflow or report.

## 2. Documentation pack (for the admin)

- Field-mapping sheet and decision log (Module 2).
- Stage definitions, the old-to-new map and the signed-off governance decisions (Module 3).
- Cleanup log with backup file names (Module 4).
- Workflow specs, the dependency map and the change log (Module 5).
- KPI definitions and the dashboard list (Module 6).
- Integration register: each integration, what it writes, owner, where its token is stored (by name only).
- A short troubleshooting page: workflow did not fire, record has no owner, duplicate created, wrong source.

## 3. Governance cadence

| When | What | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Every change | New fields, stages, workflows go through a request and are logged with date and approver | Data steward |
| Monthly | Data health review (duplicates, blanks, invalid values, unowned records), workflow errors | Data steward |
| Monthly | Short training session: dashboards, what changed, questions | Admin or implementer |
| Quarterly | Repeat the audit headline checks and compare with the baseline; retire unused fields and workflows | Admin with leadership |

## 4. Training and adoption

- Train by role: intake or SDRs on routing and lead status, AEs on the pipeline and deal rules, marketing on UTMs, sources and dashboards, leadership on reading the dashboards.
- Record short walkthroughs of common tasks; the **Meeting to Training Doc** skill turns recordings into written guides.
- Measure adoption: are reps using their saved views, are tasks being closed, are lifecycle values only changing through automation.

## 5. Close-out

- Remove the implementer's admin rights or agree in writing that they stay.
- Hand over the before and after numbers from the audit baseline.
- List the open items and the next phase (for example nurture sequences, a lead scoring model, re-engaging past customers).
