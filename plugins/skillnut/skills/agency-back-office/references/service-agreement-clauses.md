# Service Provider Agreement: balanced clause library

The fixed legal body for Module 1. Everything in `[brackets]` is filled per deal (coloured blue in drafts). Sections 3 to 16 and Exhibit 1 stay word for word unless a negotiated change is agreed. Exhibit convention: **Exhibit 1 = Mutual Confidentiality, Exhibit 2 = Statement of Work.**

Not legal advice. These are balanced commercial positions for a services agency billing B2B clients; have a lawyer adapt them for your jurisdiction.

**(Go-To-Market Services)**

The Service Provider shall provide the Services in a professional, diligent, and workmanlike manner consistent with prevailing industry standards, using its own tools and resources unless otherwise mutually agreed.

3.3 Changes to Scope. The Parties may, at any time and by mutual agreement, modify the scope of the Services during the Term through a written amendment executed by both Parties. No change to scope, fees, or timelines shall be binding unless agreed in writing by both Parties.

**4. Term, Extension and Termination**
4.1 Term. [Per deal. Shape A: This Agreement becomes effective on the Effective Date and continues for [12] months, with an evaluation point at [90] days. Shape B: This Agreement becomes effective on the Effective Date and continues for an initial pilot of [90] days, extendable by mutual written agreement based on results.] The Term may be extended or renewed by the mutual written agreement of the Parties.
4.2 Termination for Convenience. Either Party may terminate this Agreement for any reason, or no reason, upon thirty (30) days' prior written notice to the other Party.
4.3 Termination for Cause. Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure such breach within ten (10) business days after receiving written notice describing the breach in reasonable detail.
4.4 Effect of Termination. Upon termination for any reason, the Service Provider shall be entitled to compensation for all Services performed, and all approved expenses incurred, up to and including the effective date of termination. Each Party shall, within a reasonable period, return or (at the disclosing Party's option) destroy the other Party's Confidential Information and materials in its possession, except that each Party may retain copies required to comply with applicable law, professional record-keeping, or routine automated backup, provided such retained copies remain subject to the confidentiality obligations of this Agreement.
4.5 Reassignment. Upon termination, the Client may assign or transfer the work to another provider; however, the Service Provider shall not be liable for any loss, damage, or claim arising from the acts or omissions of any such other provider or third party.
4.6 Survival. Sections and obligations that by their nature should survive termination, including those relating to payment accrued, intellectual property, confidentiality, warranties, indemnification, and limitation of liability, shall survive termination or expiry of this Agreement.

**6. Expenses and Tool Costs**
6.1 Reimbursable Expenses. The Client shall reimburse reasonable, out-of-pocket expenses incurred by the Service Provider in performing the Services, provided such expenses are approved in writing by the Client in advance. The Service Provider shall submit valid receipts or supporting documentation with the applicable invoice.
6.2 Third-Party Tools. The Service Provider may recommend third-party tools, platforms, or services useful for the Services, subject to the Client's prior written approval before procurement. The Client may request that the Service Provider use the Client's existing internal tools or systems. Costs of pre-approved tools shall be borne directly by the Client or reimbursed in accordance with Section 6.1.
6.3 Infrastructure and Data Pass-Through. [Per deal: Infrastructure (domains, inboxes, sending tools) and data costs are passed through at actual cost, as estimated in Exhibit 2, Section [N]. Shape A: a one-time data sourcing estimate of approximately US$[X]. Shape B: a recurring tool budget of approximately US$[X] per month plus approximately US$[Y] one-time for domains.] The Service Provider's own subscriptions and credits are not billed to the Client.

**7. Independent Contractor Status**
7.1 Control. The Service Provider is an independent contractor and shall determine the time, place, methods, details, and means of performing the Services and delivering the Deliverables. The Client shall furnish reasonable access to facilities, personnel, information, and systems necessary to facilitate performance, and the Service Provider shall be responsible for providing the equipment, tools, know-how, and instrumentalities used in the Services.
7.2 No Employment Relationship. Nothing in this Agreement creates an employer-employee relationship, agency, partnership, or joint venture. Neither Party has authority to bind the other. The Service Provider is not eligible to participate in any employee benefit plan of the Client and is solely responsible for its own tax, labor, and legal obligations in any jurisdiction where it operates.

**8. Intellectual Property**
8.1 Client Deliverables. Subject to Section 8.2 and to full payment of the fees due for the relevant Services, all Deliverables specifically created by the Service Provider for the Client under this Agreement (the "Work Product") shall, upon such payment, be the property of the Client. The Service Provider assigns to the Client its right, title, and interest in and to such Work Product and will, at the Client's reasonable expense, execute documents reasonably necessary to perfect that assignment.
8.2 Service Provider Background IP. The Service Provider retains all right, title, and interest in and to its pre-existing and independently developed materials, tools, software, templates, workflows, frameworks, methodologies, know-how, and general skills and experience ("Background IP"), including any improvements to the foregoing that are of general application and do not embody the Client's Confidential Information. To the extent any Background IP is incorporated into the Work Product, the Service Provider grants the Client a perpetual, non-exclusive, royalty-free, worldwide license to use such Background IP solely as part of the Work Product for the Client's internal business purposes.
8.3 Third-Party Materials. Where the Deliverables incorporate third-party or open-source materials, the Client's rights in those materials are subject to the applicable third-party or open-source license terms, which the Service Provider will identify on reasonable request.

**9. Confidentiality and Data Protection**
9.1 Mutual Confidentiality. Each Party may receive confidential, proprietary, or sensitive information of the other Party. Each Party's confidentiality obligations, and the related exclusions and permitted disclosures, are set out in the Mutual Confidentiality Agreement attached as Exhibit 1, which forms part of this Agreement. The confidentiality obligations apply to both Parties on a reciprocal basis and survive for two (2) years after termination or expiry (and, for trade secrets, for as long as the information remains a trade secret under applicable law).
9.2 Data Protection. The Service Provider shall employ reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect Client data shared in connection with the Services, and shall not use, store, transmit, or disclose such data outside the scope of the Services. The Service Provider shall not retain personally identifiable information, financial data, or customer records beyond the Term without the Client's prior written approval, save for routine automated backups or as required by law. Each Party shall promptly notify the other of any confirmed data breach affecting the other's data.

**10. Representations, Warranties and Disclaimer**
10.1 Mutual. Each Party represents that it has the full right and authority to enter into and perform this Agreement and that doing so does not conflict with any other obligation binding on it.
10.2 Service Provider. The Service Provider warrants that the Services will be performed in a professional and workmanlike manner consistent with industry standards.
10.3 No Guarantee of Results. The Service Provider provides go-to-market and demand-generation services and does not warrant or guarantee any specific commercial outcome, including any particular number of leads, meetings, pipeline, conversions, revenue, deliverability, or return on investment, as such outcomes depend on numerous factors outside the Service Provider's control (including the Client's market, product, pricing, timely inputs, and third-party platforms). Except as expressly stated in this Section 10, the Services and Deliverables are provided "as is," and each Party disclaims all other warranties, whether express or implied, including implied warranties of merchantability and fitness for a particular purpose.

**11. Mutual Indemnification**
11.1 By the Service Provider. The Service Provider shall indemnify and hold harmless the Client, its officers, directors, employees, and affiliates from and against direct, third-party claims, losses, damages, or liabilities to the extent arising from (a) the Service Provider's material breach of this Agreement, or (b) the gross negligence, willful misconduct, or violation of law by the Service Provider.
11.2 By the Client. The Client shall indemnify and hold harmless the Service Provider, and its representatives, from and against direct, third-party claims, losses, damages, or liabilities to the extent arising from (a) the Client's material breach of this Agreement; (b) the Client's use, misuse, or misrepresentation of any Deliverables; (c) inaccurate, incomplete, or misleading data, materials, content, or instructions provided by the Client; or (d) the Client's violation of any law, regulation, or third-party right in connection with the engagement, including the Client's outbound communications, offers, and lists.
11.3 Procedure. The indemnified Party shall give prompt written notice of any claim, allow the indemnifying Party to control the defense and settlement (provided any settlement releasing the indemnified Party and imposing no obligation on it may be entered without consent), and reasonably cooperate at the indemnifying Party's expense.

**12. Limitation of Liability**
12.1 Cap. Except for the excluded claims in Section 12.3, each Party's total aggregate liability arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall not exceed the total fees paid or payable by the Client under this Agreement in the three (3) months immediately preceding the event giving rise to the claim.
12.2 Exclusion of Indirect Damages. In no event shall either Party be liable for indirect, incidental, special, punitive, or consequential damages, including lost profits, loss of data, loss of business, or reputational harm, even if advised of the possibility of such damages.
12.3 Exclusions. The limitations in Sections 12.1 and 12.2 do not apply to a Party's indemnification obligations under Section 11, a breach of confidentiality, amounts due and payable for Services performed, or liability that cannot be limited under applicable law.

**13. Service Provider Protections**
13.1 Non-Interference. The Client agrees not to interfere with the Service Provider's methodology, staffing, or tool choices, provided the agreed Deliverables and timelines are met. The Service Provider retains the right to determine the manner and means of performing the Services, consistent with the agreed scope and quality expectations.
13.2 Client Dependencies. If the Service Provider is unable to meet a deadline or Deliverable because the Client failed to provide timely input, approvals, assets, or access to required systems, the Service Provider shall not be liable for the resulting delay, and affected timelines shall be adjusted in good faith and confirmed in writing.
13.3 Suspension for Non-Payment. If the Client fails to pay an undisputed amount within ten (10) calendar days of the due date and does not cure within that period, the Service Provider may, on written notice, suspend the Services until all overdue amounts are paid, without being in breach of this Agreement.

**14. Non-Solicitation**
14.1 Client Customers. During the Term and for six (6) months thereafter, the Service Provider shall not knowingly and intentionally divert away from the Client any customer of the Client that the Service Provider became aware of solely through the Services. This restriction does not limit the Service Provider from providing similar go-to-market services to other clients, including in the same industry, or from serving persons or companies already known to, or independently contacting, the Service Provider.
14.2 Personnel. During the Term and for six (6) months thereafter, neither Party shall knowingly solicit for employment the other Party's personnel who were directly involved in the engagement, except through general public advertising not targeted at such personnel.

**15. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution**
15.1 Governing Law. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of [jurisdiction], without regard to its conflict-of-law principles.
15.2 Dispute Resolution. The Parties shall first attempt in good faith to resolve any dispute arising out of or relating to this Agreement through discussion between senior representatives. If not resolved within thirty (30) days, the dispute shall be finally resolved by binding arbitration seated in [seat], conducted in English, before a single arbitrator. Each Party shall bear its own costs unless the arbitrator determines otherwise. Nothing in this Section prevents a Party from seeking injunctive relief to protect its Confidential Information or intellectual property.
15.3 Attorneys' Fees. In any proceeding to interpret or enforce this Agreement, the prevailing Party shall be entitled to recover its reasonable attorneys' fees and costs.

**16. General**
16.1 Assignment. Neither Party may assign or transfer this Agreement, in whole or in part, without the other Party's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld; provided that either Party may assign this Agreement to a successor in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of substantially all of its assets, on written notice to the other Party. The Service Provider may engage qualified subcontractors to assist with the Services but remains responsible for their performance and for their compliance with this Agreement.
16.2 Notices. Notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and deemed given when sent to the registered email addresses of the Parties and acknowledged as received, or when delivered by a recognized courier to the addresses above.
16.3 Force Majeure. Neither Party shall be liable for any delay or failure to perform (other than payment obligations) due to causes beyond its reasonable control, including acts of God, internet or platform outages, government action, or labor disruption.
16.4 Entire Agreement; Amendment. This Agreement, together with Exhibits 1 and 2, constitutes the entire agreement of the Parties regarding its subject matter and supersedes all prior discussions and agreements. It is deemed jointly drafted and shall not be construed against either Party. It may be amended or waived only by a writing signed by both Parties.
16.5 Severability; Waiver. If any provision is held unenforceable, it shall be modified to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable, and the remaining provisions shall continue in full effect. No waiver is effective unless in writing signed by the waiving Party, and no waiver of one breach waives any subsequent breach.
16.6 Counterparts. This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is deemed an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.

**EXHIBIT 1 - MUTUAL CONFIDENTIALITY AGREEMENT**

This Mutual Confidentiality Agreement is entered into between [CLIENT LEGAL NAME] and [SERVICE PROVIDER NAME] (each a "Party" and, when receiving Confidential Information, the "Receiving Party," and when disclosing it, the "Disclosing Party"), and forms part of the Service Provider Agreement to which it is attached.

1. Definition of Confidential Information. "Confidential Information" means proprietary, confidential, or trade-secret information disclosed by the Disclosing Party, whether oral, written, or electronic, that is either marked or identified as confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given its nature and the circumstances of disclosure. It includes, by way of illustration and not limitation: inventions, ideas, processes, software, data, technology, know-how, designs, and techniques; research, product, business, and operational plans, budgets, financial information, pricing, and strategies; customer and prospect information, lists, and purchasing history; information about business partners, proposals, bids, and contracts; and personnel information.
2. Exclusions. Confidential Information does not include information that: (a) is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the Receiving Party; (b) was rightfully known to the Receiving Party without restriction before disclosure; (c) is rightfully obtained from a third party without breach of any obligation; or (d) is independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of or reference to the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information.
3. Obligations. The Receiving Party shall (a) use the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information solely to perform or receive the Services under the Agreement; (b) not disclose it to any third party except to its personnel or subcontractors who need to know it and are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as these; and (c) protect it using at least the same degree of care it uses for its own confidential information, and no less than a reasonable degree of care.
4. Compelled Disclosure. The Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by law or valid legal process, provided that, where legally permitted, it gives the Disclosing Party prompt notice and reasonable cooperation to seek protective treatment. [For US-law agreements: Consistent with the Defend Trade Secrets Act of 2016 (18 U.S.C. § 1833(b)), an individual shall not be held liable for disclosing a trade secret in confidence to a government official or attorney solely for the purpose of reporting or investigating a suspected violation of law, or in a sealed court filing.]
5. Return or Destruction. Upon termination of the Agreement or the Disclosing Party's written request, the Receiving Party shall return or destroy the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information, except for copies required to be retained by law, professional record-keeping, or routine automated backup, which remain subject to these confidentiality obligations for as long as they are retained.
6. Term. These confidentiality obligations apply during the Term and for two (2) years after termination or expiry of the Agreement; provided that, with respect to any information that constitutes a trade secret, the obligations continue for as long as the information remains a trade secret under applicable law.
7. No License; No Warranty. All Confidential Information remains the property of the Disclosing Party. No license or other right is granted except the limited right to use the Confidential Information to perform or receive the Services. Confidential Information is provided "as is," without warranty of accuracy or completeness.
8. Injunctive Relief. The Parties agree that a breach of this Exhibit 1 may cause irreparable harm for which monetary damages are inadequate, and that the non-breaching Party is entitled to seek injunctive relief in addition to any other available remedy.
9. General. This Exhibit 1 is governed by the same law, dispute-resolution, severability, and reformation provisions as the Agreement. If any provision is held unenforceable, it shall be modified to the minimum extent necessary and the remainder shall continue in full effect.
