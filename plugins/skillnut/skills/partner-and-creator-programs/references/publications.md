# Module 5 - Publication partners

Load this for Module 5. A foundation track: slow, separate from buyer outreach, and high leverage for being found in AI search.

## Why it matters (teach this)

- Publications are among the largest sources AI search tools cite when they answer a question. Being covered or quoted there helps the product show up in those answers.
- Discoverability and organic traffic are a publication's lifeblood. That is the angle: what you offer must help their audience and their reach, not just your launch.

## Steps

1. **Publication list.** The publications, newsletters and blogs the buyers read and that cover the category. Build it like a small TAM (the **TAM List Builder** skill), one row per publication, with a note on what they cover.
2. **Target-title map.** Research which roles own discoverability at each: the editor, the head of audience development, the growth lead for the publication or blog. Record the title found at each publication and the person, with the source.

| Publication | Covers | Discoverability owner title | Person | Source | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

3. **Messaging angle.** Write an angle distinct from the buyer messaging (which speaks to small-business or enterprise pain). The publication angle speaks to their audience and their discoverability. Draft with the **Copywriting Messaging** skill.
4. **A separate, dedicated campaign.** Never mixed into the buyer campaign. Same suppression rules (repliers out for good, 100-day cooldown on unanswered sends), same send checklist, small first wave, sent only on the user's go.

## Output

`Publication partners/`: publication list, target-title map, messaging angle with a first-touch draft, the campaign plan.
