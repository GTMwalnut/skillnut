# Module 3 - Partner recruitment

Load this for Module 3. Two motions run side by side. Both stop before any send.

| Motion | Who | How |
|---|---|---|
| A. Outbound to partners | Agencies, consultancies and freelancers who serve your buyers | The same outbound engine used for buyers, with its own list and its own copy: invite them to try the product and join as partners |
| B. Handpicked champions | A short list of high-leverage people (sources of authority, well-known implementers) | Approached personally by the program owner, one by one, coached by whoever set the program up |

## A. The outbound partner list

Build the list with the **TAM List Builder skill, partner track** (it holds the full method). The short version, so you can narrate it:
- **Your excluded rows are your partner candidates.** The agencies, consultancies and service firms your buyer ICP excludes are not buyers, so they are where partners come from. Start from the excluded and classified files of earlier buyer builds, then keyword search.
- **Classify from website proof, not labels.** Read each company's own site or description: explicit, indirect or no evidence of partner-relevant work; verdict Yes / Maybe / No; type; tier; confidence; one-line reason.
- **Competitors are not partners.** Screen them out first, using the user's competitor list; flag holding-company ownership. If there is no competitor list, mark the screen incomplete.
- **Tiers.** A = specialists whose core service is exactly where the product fits; B = broader firms with a relevant service line; C = adjacent, long tail. Solo independents in their own tab, with the privacy-law note for EU and UK sole traders.
- **People.** A few per partner company: owner or leadership first, then partnerships or business development, then the relevant service-line head. Show the full list of names before removing anyone.
- **Never overwrite a shared version.** Once a list is shared, the next one is List 2.

Then, before any send:
- Suppress against the CRM and the outreach tool with the **CRM Overlap Check** skill: past repliers out for good, unanswered sends wait 100 days, current customers and open deals out.
- Narrow and order the list with **Outbound Targeting** if it is too broad.
- Copy with **Copywriting Messaging**. Partner copy rules from real partner campaigns: write to the partner's business (more clients, a new service line, a commission), not to a buyer's pain; an open-ended opener, never "quick question"; if spintax is used, only on a few grammatical words.
- Run the **Campaign Send Checklist**, upload with the sequencer upload skill, and send only on the user's go, Tier A first, a small wave first.

Report: counts by tier and type with the reconcile line, the competitor screen (rows checked, rows removed, every removed name), the suppression result (rows checked, rows removed by reason), and the first wave proposed.

## B. Handpicked champions

- A short list, built by hand: people who already teach, implement or recommend tools in the product's space, and whose recommendation carries weight. Sources: the user's own network, people who already post about the category, customers' implementers.
- For each: name, why they matter, how they would benefit (a commission, a free plan for their clients, directory listing, early access), the warmest route in (mutual contact, a comment thread, a DM), and the owner's next step.
- The owner reaches out personally; you draft the first note per person. Each send is the owner's or needs a go.

## Output

`Partner recruitment/`: the partner list (Partner Companies, Contacts, Independents, internal tab for competitors and flags), the suppression report, the first-wave proposal, the champion sheet with a draft note each.
