# Carousel (document post) module (loaded on demand)

A carousel is a PDF uploaded as a LinkedIn document post: the reader swipes through slides. Document posts still lead other formats in reach, though the lead has shrunk (MEASURED; see the facts table in the main file). They suit anything with steps: a process, a framework, a story told in stages.

This module writes the carousel slide by slide, specifies the layout, and writes the companion post. If the platform can render files, build the PDF; otherwise hand the slide copy and the layout spec to the author's design tool.

## Pick one of three templates

| Template | Best for | Shape |
|---|---|---|
| Playbook | A repeatable process, step by step | Cover with a headline and a framework card, then one step per slide, then an end slide |
| Story | A field report: something you did and what it taught you | An opener quote, the objection, the human's role, what happened in parts, the takeaway |
| Field notes | A whole workflow at a glance | One tall infographic page (or two): numbered stages, each with two or three short lines, and one highlighted "engine room" section |

## Playbook template, slide by slide

1. **Cover.** An eyebrow ("PLAYBOOK"), a headline that names the outcome, a subtitle that says how many parts ("A 9-part playbook, starting with the ICP"), and a framework card that gives value on the cover alone (for example two columns: account criteria and people criteria). A small "swipe to begin" and the counter "01 / 09".
2. **One step per slide.** Each slide carries: a section eyebrow (the process name), a counter ("03 OF 09"), a step pill ("STEP 02"), the step name, one sentence on why the step exists, three to five short bullets, and small chips for any tools used.
3. **A "critical step" callout** on the one or two steps people skip, in plain words with the cost: "Skipping or rushing this fills your CRM with junk accounts."
4. **One concrete rule per step** where you have one ("Keep everything, exclude nothing at this stage"; "Keep the record with the most information as the parent"). These are what people save.
5. **End slide.** "End of the process", one line on what the reader now has, and a plain follow line. No keyword-comment ask.

## Story template, slide by slide

1. **Opener:** a short quote or line that is the whole story in one breath ("My only job was approving steps.").
2. **The objection:** the thing people keep saying that this story answers, in their words.
3. **The human's role:** what the person actually did, as short as possible ("Read. Judge. Approve."), with a small visual of approved and flagged items.
4. **What happened, in two or three parts,** each with roman-numeral steps, one sentence of purpose, and the trap found along the way ("a missing field is not a mismatch").
5. **The takeaway slide:** the sharpest line, then a plain follow line.

Use roman-numeral counters ("IV / VIII") for this template; it signals a story, not a manual.

## Layout spec

- **Size:** portrait 4:5 (1080 x 1350) or square (1080 x 1080). Portrait fills more of the phone screen. Keep one size per carousel.
- **Length:** 7 to 10 slides. Fewer feels thin; more loses people before the end.
- **Text:** one idea per slide, big enough to read on a phone without zooming. If a slide needs more than about 40 words, it is two slides.
- **Brand:** one consistent system across slides: either a dark header bar with one accent colour, or a light paper background with a serif headline and roman numerals. The author's photo and name on every slide (a small identity pill), so a reshared slide still carries them.
- **Before export, remove every template placeholder.** Design templates leave lines like "Drop an image here", "Click here" or "Drop identity pill" on slides; search the file for them. Treat links on slides as decoration, not as the call to action.

## The companion post

The post that carries the carousel is a normal post under the Write module rules: a first line that works alone, one real detail, plain text, no long dashes. It does not repeat the slides. It says why this process exists and the one rule that matters most, then lets the slides carry the steps. Never "comment PLAYBOOK and I'll send the PDF": the PDF is already in the post, and a keyword gate now costs about half the reach (MEASURED).

## Rules

- **Every step is real.** It comes from the author's actual process, with their actual tools. If a step is aspirational, it is not on the slide.
- **No invented numbers** on the cover or anywhere else, same as posts.
- **Sponsored or partner carousels** (a tool's features, with its logo) are marked as such in the companion post.
