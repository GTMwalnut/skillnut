# Content calendar from calls (loaded on demand)

The hardest part of posting regularly is not the writing. It is having a real detail to write about. Most founders and operators already say three post-worthy things on every call, and then forget them. This module turns a week of call transcripts into a short, scored list of post ideas on a calendar, each one traced to the call it came from.

## Inputs

- The week's call transcripts (from the meeting recorder they use, or pasted).
- The author's two or three topics. If they have none, propose them from the calls and confirm before going on.
- Where they keep the plan: a planner database, a CRM list, or a sheet. Default is a table in the reply plus a CSV.

## Method

1. **Read every transcript for idea-worthy lines.** About three per call is typical. What counts: a result with a number, a workflow that worked, a mistake and what it cost, an objection a buyer raised, a question a client asked that others will have, a before-and-after.
2. **Anonymize before anything else.** No client names, no people, no numbers that identify a client, no product names that point to them. Turn "Acme's routing broke at forty reps" into "a client's lead routing broke when the team went from twelve to forty". If a detail only works with the client named, it needs the client's written permission; flag it, do not use it.
3. **Classify against the author's topics.** Off-topic ideas go to a "parked" list, not the calendar. Focus is a real lever: distribution leans on topic match (MEASURED direction, see the facts table).
4. **Score novelty, 1 to 3.**

| Score | Meaning | Example (invented) |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | A specific only this author has: their number, their failure, a client's exact words (anonymized) | "Enrichment ran after routing, so reps got accounts with no context, and nothing got worked" |
| 2 | A known idea with a fresh real detail | "Speed-to-lead matters, and here is the 9-minute audit we ran" |
| 1 | Generic, could be anyone's | "Data quality matters" |

Only 3s and strong 2s go on the calendar. A 1 is not a post; it is a reminder that the author exists.

5. **Assign a format** using the formats table in the main file: text plus a real image for most posts, a document (carousel) post for steps and frameworks, a short vertical video if the author is good on camera. Skip polls.
6. **Schedule.** Tuesday to Thursday, business hours in the reader's timezone. Three or four a week if the specifics are there; fewer is fine, and filler to keep a streak is not. A healthy mix is roughly 70 percent tactical (how-to, what worked, the number) and 30 percent personal (what I tried, what broke). Spread across three kinds of post: authority (teach something), social proof (a result, anonymized), personal story.
7. **Write the calendar rows.** One row per idea:

| Column | What goes in it |
|---|---|
| Title | A working headline |
| Topic | One of the author's two or three |
| Format | Text and image, document, video |
| Source | The call date and type, anonymized ("client update, 14 Oct") |
| The real detail | The number, quote or moment the post stands on |
| Novelty | 1 to 3 |
| Planned date | Tue, Wed or Thu |
| Status | Idea, Draft, Review, Scheduled, Published |

8. **Draft on request** with the Write module, in the author's voice profile if one exists. Every draft carries the real detail from its row; if the row has none, it is not ready to draft.
9. **After posting, close the loop.** Mark the row Published and log impressions, comment depth (real replies, not emoji), saves and reshares. After two weeks, compare against the author's own average, not a benchmark, and keep the topics and formats that beat it.

## Model report (illustrative)

> I read your five calls from this week and found 14 lines worth a post. After anonymizing, 3 were off your two topics, so they're parked. Of the other 11, five score a 3: things only you could say, like the client whose routing broke at forty reps. I put four on the calendar for next Tuesday to Thursday: two text posts with a real screenshot, one document post for the routing fix as steps, one personal story. None of them names a client. Want me to draft Tuesday's?
