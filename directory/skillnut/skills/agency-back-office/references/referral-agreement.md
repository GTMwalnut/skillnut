# Referral Commission Agreement and the monthly commission cycle

Module 5. Written from the side of the party who **pays** commission (the "Service Provider", who receives referred clients) to the **Referrer**. Flip the roles when the user is the referrer. Not legal advice. Placeholders in `[brackets]`.

## The 17-clause skeleton

**Parties and recitals.** [Referrer legal name, form, jurisdiction, address] and [Service Provider name, form, address, tax ID if the user wants it shown]. Recitals: the Referrer has a network of potential clients; the Service Provider provides [services]; the Parties want to formalise referrals.

1. **Definitions.**
   - "Referred Client": a prospective client introduced by the Referrer in writing (email is enough) before any engagement with them.
   - "Client Contract": a signed service agreement between the Service Provider and a Referred Client.
   - "Contract Value": the monthly fees payable under a Client Contract, **excluding** one-time setup fees, out-of-pocket expenses, taxes and reimbursable costs.
   - "Commission": the referral fee in clause 4.
2. **Scope.** Non-exclusive. No minimum number of referrals; no obligation to accept any Referred Client. Independent contractors; no partnership, joint venture, agency or employment.
3. **Referral process.**
   - The Referrer shares name, contact details and a short description in writing.
   - The Service Provider acknowledges within **5 business days**.
   - A referral is valid for **6 months**; it lapses if no Client Contract is signed by then, unless extended in writing.
   - If the Service Provider already knows or is talking to the prospect, it says so within **5 business days**, and no commission is due unless agreed otherwise.
4. **Commission.**
   - Up to **[X]%** of Contract Value per month, for as long as the Client Contract is active and the client is paying.
   - The exact rate per referral is agreed in writing (email is enough) at or before the referral; default **[X]%**.
   - Payable **only on cash actually received** from the Referred Client. Nothing on unpaid, disputed or written-off amounts.
   - Recalculated from the date the Contract Value changes.
   - Ends when the Client Contract ends, or when this agreement ends (subject to the tail period).
5. **Payment terms.**
   - The Service Provider updates a shared tracking sheet with each month's earned commission by **business day 3** of the next month.
   - The Referrer invoices within **10 days** of the start of the next month.
   - The Service Provider pays within **5 business days** of a valid, undisputed invoice, provided the client has paid for that period.
   - Invoice concerns are raised within **15 days**; undisputed parts are paid on time.
   - Currency and method: [e.g. USD by wire]. Bank details supplied by the Referrer (never pre-filled from another agreement). The Referrer may update them on reasonable written notice.
   - Tax: each party handles its own. [If the Referrer has no presence in the Service Provider's country, state the parties' understanding of withholding and indirect tax, confirmed with an accountant.]
6. **Term and termination.** **1 year**, auto-renewing for 1-year terms unless either party gives **30 days'** notice before expiry. Either party may terminate on **30 days'** notice. **Tail period:** after termination, commission continues on Client Contracts signed during the term, for the rest of those contracts or **6 months**, whichever is shorter.
7. **Confidentiality.** Client details, pricing, strategy and the agreement's terms; no sharing except as required by law or with bound advisors; survives **2 years**.
8. **Representations and warranties.** Authority to sign; referrals are bona fide; the Service Provider delivers professionally and keeps accurate payment and commission records.
9. **Non-circumvention.** No structuring deals with Referred Clients to avoid commission; during the term and **12 months** after.
10. **Liability.** No indirect or consequential damages; no guarantee of a Referred Client's quality or creditworthiness; each party responsible for its own services.
11. **Data protection.** Personal data shared for referrals used only for this agreement, under applicable privacy law.
12. **Compliance.** Anti-bribery and anti-corruption; no improper payments.
13. **Force majeure.** Including pandemics and telecom failures; prompt notice; either party may terminate after **60 days**, with accrued commission still due.
14. **Dispute resolution.** Good-faith talks for **30 days**, then binding arbitration by a sole arbitrator, [seat], [language], virtual hearings allowed; final and binding.
15. **Governing law.** [Jurisdiction]; disputes only through clause 14 except interim relief where the law requires.
16. **Notices.** In writing, by email, registered post or courier, to the addresses in the notice table; either party may update them in writing.
17. **General.** Entire agreement (with the annexure); written amendments; no waiver; severability; assignment only with consent except merger or acquisition where the buyer is bound; counterparts and e-signatures valid; survival of clauses 7, 9, 10, 14 and 15.

Signature table: for each party, signature, name, title, date, place.

## Monthly cycle: paying a referrer

1. For each Referred Client with an active Client Contract: monthly Contract Value received this month (exclude setup fees, expenses, taxes).
2. Commission = agreed rate x that amount. Nothing for months the client hasn't paid.
3. Update the shared tracking sheet by business day 3.
4. Wait for the referrer's invoice; check it against the sheet.
5. Pay within 5 business days of a valid invoice.
6. Log the payment date and reference.
