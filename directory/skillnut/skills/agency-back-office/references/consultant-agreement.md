# Independent Consultant Services Agreement (both directions)

Module 4. One skeleton, two directions:
- **HIRE**: the user engages a contractor (the user is the "Client").
- **SERVE**: the user is the consultant to someone else's business (the user is the "Consultant").

Not legal advice. Placeholders in `[brackets]`.

## Client Protections (HIRE)

a. **Performance Standards.** The Consultant agrees to meet agreed deliverables and timelines. Consistent failure to meet performance standards may result in termination for cause under Section 2.
b. **Non-Compete During Engagement.** During the term, the Consultant shall not provide similar services to direct competitors of the Client without prior written disclosure and consent.
c. **Return of Materials.** On termination or expiry, the Consultant shall promptly return or destroy all Client materials, data, documents and Confidential Information.
d. **Payment Contingent on Deliverables.** Payment for monthly services is contingent on satisfactory completion of agreed deliverables. The Client may withhold payment proportional to incomplete or substandard work, subject to written notice and an opportunity to cure.

## Consultant Protections (SERVE)

a. **Non-Interference.** The client won't interfere with the consultant's methods, staffing or tool choices while deliverables and timelines are met.
b. **Client Dependencies.** No liability for delays caused by late client inputs, approvals or access; timelines move in good faith, confirmed in writing.
c. **Suspension for Non-Payment.** The consultant may pause work on written notice until overdue undisputed amounts are paid.
d. **Limitation of Liability.** The consultant's total liability is capped at the fees paid in the three months before the claim; no indirect, incidental or consequential damages (lost profits, data, reputation).

**Execution scope (both directions):**
1. Lead sourcing with ICP filters (industries, size, titles, exclusions) using data tools.
2. Domains and inboxes set up correctly; DNS records and warm-up.
3. ICP research and analysis; validate and pivot on responses.
4. Message-market fit: messaging per persona and pain point, refined on reply rates.
5. Running campaigns on email and LinkedIn, expandable to other channels.
6. Campaign dashboards and data.
7. Miscellaneous advice: tool audit to reduce costs, website and LinkedIn messaging, advanced workflows.

**Extra for HIRE (senior contractor):**
8. GTM strategy and systems architecture.
9. CRM and RevOps setup and hygiene.
10. Reporting requirements: what is reported, how often, in what format.
11. Communication expectations: channels, response times, meeting attendance.
12. Quality standards: what "done" means for each deliverable (for example lists checked before upload, copy reviewed before launch).

## Monthly (HIRE)

Collect the contractor's invoice at month end, check it against the deliverables and the hours log if one is required, pay within the agreed days, and file it as a cost (inward supply) with your other spend records.
