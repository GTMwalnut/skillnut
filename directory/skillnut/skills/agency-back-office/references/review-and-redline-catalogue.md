# Review benchmark and redline catalogue

Used by Module 2 (Redline) and Module 3 (Review). The "balanced position" column is the clause library in `service-agreement-clauses.md`. Not legal advice.

## Benchmark: score every clause against this

| Topic | Green (balanced) | Amber (livable, push back) | Red (one-sided; counter or walk) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Termination for convenience | Either party, 30 days' notice | 60+ days, or only one party may terminate | You can't leave; they can leave at will |
| Termination for cause | Written notice + 10 business days to cure | Shorter cure, or "proven" breach required | Immediate termination with no cure |
| Payment due | 10 days or less | 30 days | 45+ days, or "on client's payment run" |
| Invoice disputes | Raised within a few days or deemed accepted | No deadline but good faith | Client can withhold everything over any dispute |
| Late payment / non-payment | Interest + right to suspend work | Suspension only | No remedy at all |
| Fees tied to results | Retainer, or retainer + clearly defined per-meeting fee | Per-meeting fee with a loose definition | Pay only on results you don't control |
| IP | Client owns what you make for them, on payment; you keep background IP and license it | Client owns deliverables before payment | Everything you create, including methods, templates and tools, is assigned to them |
| Prior inventions schedule | Your background materials are listed | Left blank | Says "none" |
| Non-compete | Narrow non-solicit of their customers you met only through them, 6 months | Non-compete limited to named direct competitors during the term | Industry-wide non-compete, or after the term ends |
| Competitor list exhibit | Not present, or names only current direct-competitor work | Long list you must keep updated | Blocks the clients you plan to sign |
| Confidentiality | Mutual, 2 years, trade secrets longer | One-way (only you are bound) | Perpetual for everything |
| Results | Workmanlike standard, explicit no-guarantee-of-results | Silent | You guarantee meetings, pipeline, revenue or deliverability |
| Liability | Mutual cap at last 3 months' fees, no indirect damages, sensible carve-outs | Cap at 12 months' fees | No cap |
| Indemnity | Mutual; client covers its data, lists, offers and outreach compliance | Only you indemnify, but narrowly | You indemnify for everything, including their data |
| Policies by reference | None | Code of conduct and data security policy only | Whole employee handbook (hours, discipline, benefits rules) |
| Renewal | Mutual written extension | Auto-renewal with a 30-day opt-out | Auto-renewal with no notice window |
| Image and voice rights (content, creator deals) | Licensed for the named campaign, limited time and channels | Broad channels, limited time | Perpetual, worldwide, all media, assignable |
| Exclusivity | None | Exclusive for one named product category during the term | Exclusive across your whole service line |
| Governing law | Your jurisdiction, arbitration | Theirs, with arbitration | Theirs, in their courts only |
| Assignment | Mutual consent, successor carve-out | They may assign freely, you may not | They may assign to anyone including a competitor |

## Always flag (even if everything else is green)

1. Unlimited liability.
2. IP assignment that captures your methods, templates, workflows or tools.
3. A competitor list or exclusivity that blocks future clients.
4. An employee handbook or policy incorporated by reference.
5. Automatic renewal without a notice window.
6. Perpetual, worldwide image and voice rights.
7. A "prior inventions" schedule left as "none". Fill it: list your playbooks, templates, scripts, automations and frameworks as background IP.

## Redline catalogue: kinds of asks and the usual answer

| Client ask | Usual answer | Wording or condition |
|---|---|---|
| Their home-country law and that country's arbitration institute | Accept, with a lawyer from that country reading the final | Keep arbitration (not courts), single arbitrator, English, remote hearings allowed |
| Add a data-processing addendum (DPA) | Accept as a new Exhibit 3 | Make sure it describes the data you actually touch (prospect contact data, CRM fields), and the sub-processors you use |
| A lock-in for the first month | Accept | "Neither Party may terminate for convenience with effect before the end of the first month" |
| A seasonal slowdown clause | Accept with a floor | Reduced activity in named months, at a named reduced retainer, agreed in writing a month ahead |
| Lower retainer after month one | Counter: lower only if scope drops to match | Name what stops (for example list building done, only running campaigns) |
| A monthly tool-cost cap | Accept, but exclude tools the client chose or asked for by name | "Pass-through tool costs will not exceed US$[X] per month, excluding [tool], without the Client's written approval" |
| Read access to the client's CRM to count meetings | Accept; it protects you too | Read access to deal and meeting records only |
| Activity logged in the client's CRM | Accept | State which activities and how often, so it isn't open-ended admin |
| Automations built in the client's own workflow tool | Accept | Client provides admin access; you deliver documentation and a handover session at the end |
| Handover of sending-tool data at the end | Accept | Exports of lists, sequences and results; not your account, templates or other clients' data |
| LinkedIn method and platform-terms compliance | Accept with a shared-responsibility line | You use the client's accounts as directed; the client confirms it accepts the platform's terms for its own accounts |
| How a per-meeting fee treats existing accounts, inbound leads, repeat meetings | Clarify in 5.1 | Existing customers, open opportunities and inbound leads don't count; a second meeting with the same company in the same month doesn't count |
| Longer invoice dispute window | Counter in the middle | For example 5 business days instead of 3 calendar days |
| Measurable review criteria | Accept, with numbers | Month 3: setup items live, N meetings held, reply rate above X%. Month 6: pipeline value or meetings above Y |
| Full rescope (for example retainer to a one-time project) | Rebuild 4.1, 5.1, 6.3 and the statement of work | Remove every leftover of the old scope from the statement of work (search for the old service names) |
| Signatory title correction | Accept | Search every occurrence, including tables and the signature block |
| Replace vague "ICP filters" input | Do it yourself before they ask | A "Lead list filters" list: industries, revenue or size buckets, titles, exclusion keywords, regions, suppressions |
